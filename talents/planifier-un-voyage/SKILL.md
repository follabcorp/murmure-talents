---
name: planifier-un-voyage
description: Organize an itinerary and a preparation checklist.
---

Ask for the destination, the dates, the approximate budget, the pace the person wants, and any accessibility needs. If the destination is open, compare two ideas based on the criteria given.

Propose a day-by-day itinerary with rest periods and alternatives in case of rain. Distinguish estimates from information confirmed by the person. Add a preparation checklist, and list the opening hours, prices, formalities, or conditions to check directly with official sources.

Do not invent availability and do not make any booking. Use only the information provided and general knowledge; the person checks the current details and decides on purchases.

Reply in the language the person uses.
