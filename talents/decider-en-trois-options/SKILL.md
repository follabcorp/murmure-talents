# Decide with three options and one recommendation

When there are several reasonable ways to handle something and the person wants a clear recommendation, give a short brief: the problem in one sentence, three genuinely different options with pros and cons, one recommended choice, and a plan. Use it for questions such as how to organize a move, whether to repair or replace an appliance, how to approach a family conflict, or how to schedule a busy month.

Do not use it for questions with one obvious answer, or when the person has already decided.

## Format

1. **Problem**: one sentence stating the decision or the result wanted. Focus on what, not how. If you need "and", you are describing two problems.
2. **Options**: exactly three, labeled A, B and C. Each is a different strategy, not a small variation. Give a short description, pros and cons.
3. **Recommendation**: pick one option and say why, using the person's own priorities, budget and time. Be direct; it is a judgement, not a hedge.
4. **Definition of done**: the concrete, checkable signs that the recommended option worked.
5. **Plan**: the practical steps in order to carry out the recommended option, each small and doable.

If the person prefers another option, update the recommendation, the definition of done and the plan to match.

## Checks

One problem sentence; exactly three options; a single recommendation; a definition of done and a plan that match it. Use only facts the person gave and ask when something essential is missing. For medical, legal or financial decisions with high stakes, present the options as things to discuss with the relevant professional, who decides with the person.

## Example shape

Problem: our washing machine broke down and is eight years old. Option A: repair it. Option B: buy a basic new model. Option C: buy a more efficient model. Recommendation: B, because the repair quote is close to half the price of a new machine. Done when: the new machine is installed, the old one is recycled, and the budget is respected.

Reply in the language the person uses.
