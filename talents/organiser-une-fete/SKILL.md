# Organize a party

Move from the idea to a usable plan for the day: budget, venue, guests, deadlines, suppliers, schedule, and fallback solutions. Suits meals, birthdays, weddings, professional gatherings, and association events.

## Useful information

Gather the type of event, its purpose, and the desired atmosphere; the date or how flexible it is; the number and profile of guests; the total budget and what it covers; the region and the venue considered, indoor or outdoor. Note the must-haves, accessibility needs, children, dietary needs, and risks already known, notably weather and travel.

## Process

1. Confirm in a few lines the type, date, headcount, budget, and atmosphere. Date, headcount, budget, and venue determine what follows.
2. Split the budget across the main items and set aside **10 to 15%** for unforeseen costs. For a wedding or a large event, the venue and catering can exceed half the total; treat this as an estimate to check.
3. Size the venue: capacity, layout, transport, parking, toilets, sound and image, and food on site or outside. Plan a covered solution or another venue for outdoor events.
4. Prepare the guest list, the invitations, a reply date, and a tracking table for the person to fill in. Ask for dietary and accessibility needs as soon as guests reply. Estimate possible absences without exceeding capacity if everyone comes.
5. Work backward from the date to today: choice of venue, invitations, menu, confirmations, final headcount, and preparations on the day.
6. List the suppliers and tasks: food, drinks, cake, decoration, sound, photography, music, rentals, and transport. For each, plan a quote, a deposit, a confirmation, and a responsible contact.
7. Write a precise hour-by-hour schedule: set-up, welcome, meal, speeches, key moments, and clean-up. Assign a person in charge to each segment.
8. Plan for rain, a supplier's absence, extra food, a person in charge on the day, and a repair kit. Confirm services and headcount **48 to 72 hours before**.

## Indicative lead times

| Event | Main items | Lead time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dinner for up to twelve people | Food, drinks | One to three weeks |
| Birthday for twenty to fifty people | Venue, meal, cake, entertainment | Three to six weeks |
| Wedding | Venue, caterer, photography | Six to twelve months |
| Professional gathering | Venue, travel, meal, entertainment | One to three months |
| Conference or association fundraiser | Venue, sound, meal, speakers, permits | Three to nine months |

Decide the main items early, and give the running of the day to someone other than the host. Plan set-up and clean-up, which are often underestimated. Include taxes, service charges, possible tips, and rentals in the budget. Check parking, toilets, electricity, access, and meal times.

This Talent prepares documents and lists; it does not book or contact suppliers. Prices, capacities, quotes, and contracts must be confirmed directly. For an important or risky event, plan for professional help and the local checks required: permits, alcohol, and public safety.

Reply in the language the person uses.
