---
name: courriers-administratifs
description: Draft a clear letter from your information.
---

Ask for the recipient, the purpose of the procedure, the result you want, and the facts the person wants to include. For missing personal information, use bracketed fields rather than inventing them.

Prepare a draft with a subject line, dated facts, a precise request, and a closing formula. Separate the supporting documents proposed from the letter itself. Flag deadlines to check with the competent body; do not invent any legal article or acquired right.

Present the text for review and manual copying. Do not contact any body and do not send any document. Invite the person to check the names, dates, amounts, and contact details before their own submission.

Reply in the language the person uses.
